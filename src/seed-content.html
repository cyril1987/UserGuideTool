System Overview

The Invoice Processing system provides end-to-end management of vendor and customer invoices. Here is an overview of the key modules you will work with:

🔍

Invoice Search

Find any invoice using flexible filters — by type, date, status, amount, or custom parameters.

✅

Workflow Approval

Multi-level approval queues. Assign, review, approve, or request changes on invoices.

🔗

Reconciliation

Match billed amounts (LIDs) against expected amounts (EBLs) to catch discrepancies.

📊

Accounting

Automatic account resolution, posting, and error handling. View or change account segments.

❌

Rejection Handling

Full/partial rejection, outstanding rejection documents, and credit note processing.

📋

Reports

Generate unapproved, approved, workflow trail, reconciliation, detail, and expense reports.

5.8 Invoice Auto Approvals

When Are Invoices Auto-Approved?

Not all invoices require manual approval. The system can auto-approve invoices when reconciliation results meet configured thresholds:

Tolerance Match at Recon Level: If a service has a configured tolerance (e.g., 2% for "Deicing Equipment"), LIDs within that range become "Tol-Matched". If Matched + Tol-Matched + Recon-Exempt = 100%, the invoice is automatically approved.

5.9 Amount Level Tolerance

A separate tolerance can be set at the total invoice amount level:

💡

7Pay Full & Pay Accepted Accounting

What you'll learn: The two payment models determine how rejections are financially handled. Understanding this is crucial for correct accounting treatment.

Pay Full vs. Pay Accepted — Side by Side

Pay FullPay Accepted
Payment timingPay full invoice amount immediatelyPay only accepted (non-rejected) amount
PostingFull amount posted to ERP; rejected portion goes to suspense accountOnly accepted amount posted to ERP
If vendor accepts rejectionVendor issues Credit Note → link to OR → suspense account clearedCredit Note linked to OR → both posted; or just close OR if no CN
If vendor rejects rejectionReverse OR → JV created to book expense from suspense to expense accountReverse OR → payment released for the additional amount
⚠️
Key difference: In Pay Full, you've already paid everything — so reversals move money from suspense to expense. In Pay Accepted, you haven't paid the disputed amount — so reversals trigger additional payment.

8Outstanding Rejection Document Actions

What you'll learn: After rejection, the system auto-generates an Outstanding Rejection (OR) document. This section covers how to view, reverse, or close these documents.
📋
Naming convention: Outstanding rejection documents follow the pattern [InvoiceNumber]-OR-[#]. For example, if invoice INV0002341 is rejected, the first OR document will be INV0002341-OR-1.

8.1 View Outstanding Rejection

  1. Go to Invoice Search → Vendor Invoices
  2. Find the rejected invoice → click the view icon
  3. In Invoice Summary, click 'View Details'
  4. Click the OR document link
  5. View the Outstanding Rejection document

8.2 Reversing OR — Pay Full Scenario

When the vendor doesn't accept your rejection (Pay Full), you need to reverse the OR to move the amount from suspense to expense:

  1. Open the OR from workflow after assigning to self
  2. Click "Reverse Outstanding Rejection"
  3. Enter reversal amounts per service → click 'Reverse'
  4. The OR moves out of workflow and a Journal Voucher (JV) document is auto-created

8.3 Reversing OR — Pay Accepted Scenario

Same process as Pay Full — click "Reverse Outstanding Rejection".

8.4 Closing OR — Pay Accepted Scenario

When the vendor accepts the rejection but doesn't issue a credit note (Pay Accepted), simply close the OR since no financial adjustment is needed.

Frequently Asked Questions

I can't approve an invoice — what should I check?
Ensure the invoice is assigned to you. Click the "Assign to Me" button (person icon) in the workflow dashboard first. Only then will the Process/Cancel buttons appear when you view the document.
What's the difference between "Matched" and "Tolerance Matched"?
Matched means the LID and EBL amounts are exactly equal. Tolerance Matched (Tol-Matched) means there's a small difference within a pre-configured acceptable range. Both are considered acceptable for auto-approval.
I need to fix an accounting error — what's the quickest path?
Go to the invoice → scroll to Accounting section → click "View Errors" → select the Error Group → click "Change Account Value" to fix → then Re-trigger Accounting. See Sections 5.4.5 and 4.3.
Can I undo a rejection after processing?
Before processing: Yes — use the "Revert Rejection" button (Section 6.3).
After processing: The rejection creates an Outstanding Rejection document. You can reverse it through the OR workflow (Section 8.2/8.3).
When should I choose "Pay Full" vs "Pay Accepted"?
This depends on your organization's payment policy. Pay Full: You pay the entire invoice immediately, then handle disputes after. Pay Accepted: You only pay what you agree with. See Section 7 for detailed implications.
How do I find invoices with accounting errors?
Use Invoice Search with the "Account Posting Status" filter set to "Error". See Section 3.
What happens to an invoice after it reaches FINAL status?
Once an invoice reaches FINAL status, it leaves the workflow and is no longer visible in the workflow dashboard. You can still find it through Invoice Search.
Why are some LIDs "Unreconciled" and what should I do?
Unreconciled LIDs couldn't be matched with any EBLs. Try Re-trigger Recon after EBLs are generated, try Manual Linking (Section 5.7), or investigate with the vendor.
Can I download any report from the system?
Yes. All reports support .csv and .xlsx download via the download button at the bottom of each report screen.
